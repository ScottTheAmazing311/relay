# Relay Workplace Logistics

Brand and website for Relay, a (fictional/in-development) commercial office moving
company: furniture decommission, transport, storage, reassembly. Tagline:
"Out Friday. Open Monday."

## Map

- `index.html` — the whole site: a single static page, no build step. Deploys on
  Vercel from `main` (framework "Other", no build command).
- `img/` — photography (owner-supplied) and `logo.png` (the logo, background
  removed, recolored to brand ink).
- `brand/BRAND.md` — the brand book: voice, palette, type, logo rules. Read it
  before changing any visual or copy.
- `brand/logo-original.png` — the owner's original logo artwork. NEVER redraw or
  re-vectorize the logo; the owner rejected rebuilt versions. Pixel operations
  (recolor, background removal) are acceptable; shape changes are not.
- `brand/concepts/` — the rejected logo concept round, kept for history.

## Hard rules

- Textured white is the only theme (no dark variant). No blue anywhere: ink is
  slate #232B32, accent is Signal Amber #F2A900, dark surfaces are near-neutral.
- Headings: Bitter (slab), sentence case. Body: Source Sans 3. No AI-design
  tropes (no eyebrow chips, numbered section markers, dark hero + accent pop,
  giant uppercase fragments, gradient heroes).
- Phone, email, USDOT number, and the quote form are placeholders; the footer
  says so. Keep that honesty until real details exist.
- A live artifact preview exists; the repo is canonical.
